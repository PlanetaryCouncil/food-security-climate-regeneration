# Food security, climate and regeneration (UK)

**Can Britain Feed Itself? Store and grow.** A ~14-page briefing on UK food resilience. **The benefit first:** safety and stability for about £10 a person a year, insuring a food sector worth £162bn a year of value added and 4.1 m jobs for under 0.5% of that value. Two equally important pillars:

- **STORE:** the **UK Strategic Food Reserve**, about £10 per person per year. Human food first and food for everyone: bread from 4 Mt of food-grade wheat, a no-cook ration and vitamin tablets for all, plus seed, safe nitrogen, protein feed and imported staples. It is run by a lean, open, automated agency.
- **GROW:** **domestic resilience**, about £4.50 per person per year of new money plus better-aimed farm support. It covers soil, water, seed, fertiliser, home-grown protein and regenerative farming.

Both pillars create work: up to ~3,600 gross, mostly rural jobs, with farming as a prestigious career and Britain growing a surplus of what it grows best. A contingency ladder (fair rationing, livestock, safe nitrogen) sits above both. A related, uncosted concept, **Homes, Land and Livelihoods** (garden villages and new towns with shared land), follows the plan.
